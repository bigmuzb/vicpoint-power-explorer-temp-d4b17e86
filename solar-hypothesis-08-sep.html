Purpose: compare the shape of measured phase current with a deliberately simple 90 A-per-phase solar hypothesis. The curve begins at sunrise, clips at the assumed inverter ceiling around the middle of the day, and reaches zero at sunset.
Do not add this curve directly to the measured amps and call it site usage. SL1 current is unsigned magnitude. Import/export direction, phase angle and actual inverter phase allocation are unknown. This is a visual hypothesis only.
Sun times: civil dawn 05:29, sunrise 05:52, solar noon 11:45, sunset 17:38 and civil dusk 18:00. Source: sunrise-sunset.org.
Phase-awareness: a conventional three-phase inverter generally balances its own generation across phases; it does not automatically repair the site's load balance. Some hybrid systems support unbalanced output, so the inverter models and configuration must be checked.
