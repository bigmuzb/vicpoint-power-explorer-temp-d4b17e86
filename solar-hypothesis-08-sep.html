Victoria Point — solar-adjusted current hypothesis

The phase traces are measured SL1 current magnitude, smoothed to five-minute medians. The yellow trace is a deliberately modelled solar envelope only: sunrise 05:52, 90 A assumed inverter ceiling, sunset 17:38.

Not measured gross consumption. The archived logger did not record signed phase watts. The orange line is the requested simple “solar + measured current” estimate; the shaded range shows how wide the answer can be when a phase may be importing or exporting. New SL1 signed-watts logging removes this ambiguity.
